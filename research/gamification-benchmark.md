# Gamification benchmark: scam awareness and financial literacy games

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Purpose: learn what makes existing games work, borrow the proven mechanics, and find
the gap Celik fills.

## 1. Products analysed

| Product | Who / where | Core mechanic | What makes it unique | Evidence of impact | What Celik borrows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bad News | Cambridge Social Decision-Making Lab + DROG (2018) | Player becomes a fake-news producer and earns 6 badges, one per manipulation technique | Inoculation / prebunking: a weak dose of manipulation builds resistance | 15,000-player study: 15 minutes of play measurably raised resistance; effect lasted at least 3 months with follow-up testing | Mod Sindiket: play the scammer, one badge per tactic, debrief after each |
| #XiamTheScams | Singapore NCPC + SPF + MHA, built by Sqkii (Oct 2024 - Jan 2025) | Life simulator: choose a character, face scam and legit everyday situations, balance health, wealth, happiness | Mixes real and fake opportunities; rewards real-world protective actions; cash prizes and leaderboard | 519,000+ players, 10M+ scenarios played, 77,000+ real anti-scam actions (app downloads, alert subscriptions) | Scam + legit mix (so players judge, not just reject); "Book of Scams" collection idea; reward real actions |
| Interland (Be Internet Awesome) | Google + North Kingdom (2017) | 4 themed islands of short arcade games; Reality River is a phishing quiz you must answer to cross | Short, themed levels with a world map; works on any browser | Used in schools with a teacher curriculum | Short levels, friendly guide character (our Sang Kancil coach) |
| Spent | Urban Ministries of Durham + McKinney (2011) | Survive a month on USD 1,000; every choice shows its consequence | Empathy through hard trade-offs; consequences explained at every step | Played 2M+ times in 196 countries | Dompet: semester budget where choices have visible consequences |
| Zogo | US fintech, distributed by credit unions | Bite-sized finance lessons, hearts, streaks, points ("pineapples") redeemable for gift cards | Partners pay real rewards for learning | Hundreds of credit unions offer it to members | Partner reward model (roadmap: banks, AKPK) |
| Online Safety IRL: Scam Edition | Malaysia, supported by the Communications Ministry, run by Ratio:Cause | Creator-led campaign plus an interactive scam-spotting simulation | Youth advocates and creators drive reach | 18.3M people reached online; 900,000+ users in the simulation | Proof Malaysians engage with simulations; creator-led launch idea |
| Mind Your Ringgit | Visa Malaysia, supporting the Financial Education Network (BNM + SC), launched 2021 | One-year web simulation: a financial decision each month based on real-life events | Covers digital payments, scams, insurance, investments, loans and savings in one game; BM and English | Backed by national financial education bodies; AKPK endorsed it | Closest Malaysian competitor: shows judges know money + scam sims; Celik must stand out with student life, mule accounts and playing the scammer |
| Meta "Is This Legit?" | Meta (Malaysia) | Scam-spotting game | Platform-owned distribution | 26,000+ Malaysian players | Quick-spot format (our Sahih atau Scam) |
| SPF "Scam Me If You Can" / "ScamBat" / arcade | Singapore Police Force | Kiosk tile-matching game with quiz power-ups; card game; arcade machine | Physical, event-based play at campuses and roadshows | Deployed at a university and libraries | Event mode for orientation weeks (our Mod Kelas) |
| Duolingo | Language app (well known) | Daily streaks, XP, leagues, short lessons | Habit loop | Widely studied | Daily Sahih atau Scam set with streak |
| Kahoot | Classroom quiz platform (well known) | Live quiz on a big screen, teams, leaderboard | Group energy in one room | Widely used in schools and universities | Mod Kelas projector battles |

## 2. Patterns that work

1. **Experience beats explanation.** Bad News, Spent and #XiamTheScams all make the player choose and live with the result.
2. **Teach tactics, not cases.** Bad News targets techniques, so learning transfers to new scams. Scams change every month; tactics don't.
3. **Mix legit with fake.** #XiamTheScams includes real opportunities, so players learn judgement instead of paranoia.
4. **Short sessions.** All the successful ones take 5-15 minutes.
5. **Convert to real action.** #XiamTheScams counted real protective steps, not just scores.
6. **Collections and badges** give a reason to replay (Bad News badges, #XiamTheScams Book of Scams).

## 3. The gap Celik fills

- No game found targets **university students and mule-account recruitment** in Malaysia.
- No Malaysian scam game combines **defending (life sim) with attacking (scammer role-play)**.
- Mind Your Ringgit covers money and scams as separate topics in one simulation; we found no game where a weak
  budget makes the next scam **harder to resist** (being broke makes scam offers more tempting).
- None is designed for **orientation week**, a natural moment when every first-year is in one hall.

## 4. New mechanics proposed for Celik

| Mechanic | How it plays | Why it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semester Pertama | 12-week campus life sim in a fake phone: chats, calls, ads, bank app | Realistic context; consequences are personal |
| Desperation effect | Low savings raise Tekanan; desperate offers look more tempting | Shows why broke students get recruited as mules |
| Money-chain reveal | After a mule choice, animation follows the victim's money through your account to the syndicate and the police back to you | Makes the invisible mule mechanism visible |
| Tap the red flags | After each scenario, hidden red-flag phrases glow; tap to collect cards | Active recall of tactics |
| Imuniti per tactic | Score per tactic; adaptive engine serves your weakest tactic next | Personal, targeted practice |
| Mod Sindiket | Build scam messages from Hook + Tactic + Ask cards; watch a success meter; earn badges; debrief | Inoculation (Bad News evidence) |
| Sindiket twist | The syndicate collapses; interns' mule accounts get traced | Shows the scam ends badly for everyone in the chain |
| "Terlalu curiga?" check | Rejecting real opportunities costs missed income | Judgement, not paranoia |
| Latihan Tindakan | 997 call drill, Semak Mule simulation, real-action checklist with stars | Converts knowledge into real protective steps |
| Mod Kelas | Projector team battles for orientation halls and classes | Group learning; fits MOHE awareness programmes |
| Imuniti share card | Shareable result image | Viral spread among students |

## 5. Financial literacy and retention benchmark (added 6 Nov 2026 evening, decision D18)

| Product | Target user | Core mechanic | What brings users back | Evidence | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mind Your Ringgit (Visa MY) | Young Malaysians | One-year web sim, a money decision each month | Monthly decisions | Pilot then public launch; no outcome data found | Generic adults; scams one topic of many; no student context |
| Belanjawanku (KWSP) | Working adults | Budget tracker with an expense guide | Real tracking (a chore) | About 12,000 downloads by May 2023 | Useful but not fun; low uptake |
| InvestSmart (SC) | Retail investors | Fairs, talks, free financial planning sessions | Yearly events | 12th annual InvestSmart Fest in 2025 | Event-based, not a daily product |
| Versa (MY investing app) | Young investors | Real saving and investing with "Versa Quest" | Real money and returns | 300,000+ users | Commercial; teaches using a product, not judgement |
| Zogo (US) | Gen Z | Bite-sized lessons, points redeemable for rewards | Rewards paid by partner banks and credit unions | ~15M lessons, ~1M users (2021) | Needs partners to fund rewards |
| Duolingo (pattern) | Learners | Short daily lessons | Streaks | Going from a 1- to 2-day streak is a big jump in retention | Not finance |
| #XiamTheScams (SG) | Public | Life sim, 90 storylines over 3 months | Prizes, leaderboard, new storylines | 519k players | Retention bought with S$55,000 prizes; time-limited |
| Bad News (UK) | Public | Play the manipulator | None (one play) | Works, but decays within 2 months without boosters | One-time use |

### What this changes

- **Retention comes from mechanics, not topic breadth**: streaks (Duolingo), real rewards (Zogo, #XiamTheScams),
  fresh content seasons (#XiamTheScams) and institutional channels (Zogo via credit unions). Broad financial
  literacy tools in Malaysia (Belanjawanku, Mind Your Ringgit) have not shown strong uptake on their own.
- **Celik's gap now**: the student money moment + real vs fake investing side by side (kawan_labur vs
  crypto_naga) + scams as the consequence of low financial literacy + a daily booster (Cek Harian) + an
  institutional channel that repeats every intake (Mod Kelas at orientation).
