# Validation test: 12 questions (D3)

The before/after test from docs/SRS.md 5.3: 6 money questions and 6 scam-spotting questions. Each one checks a
concept or tactic that the game teaches, so a change in score can be traced back to the game. The same paper is used
before and after one play session (see `participants.md`); the scores go in `results.md` (D4).

**Rules for this paper:** no names or personal details on the paper, only a random code (hard rule 2). Every brand,
link and phone number is fictional (`.test` links, `01X-0000 0NNN` numbers); NSRC 997 is the only real item, as in the
game (hard rule 1). Rates are examples, not advice (hard rule 12).

**Review:** Danish checks accuracy (comments only, section "Review notes" at the bottom). Aimar makes the changes.

Pilih satu jawapan untuk setiap soalan. Ini bukan peperiksaan; jawab ikut apa yang kamu tahu sekarang.

### Bahagian A: Duit / Part A: Money

**1.** Elaun kamu RM600 sebulan. Cara mana paling baik untuk mula berbelanja?
Your allowance is RM600 a month. Which is the best way to start spending?
- A. Belanja dulu, simpan apa yang tinggal di hujung bulan / Spend first, save whatever is left at the end of the month
- B. Asingkan simpanan dulu, kemudian bahagikan baki kepada keperluan dan kehendak / Set savings aside first, then split the rest into needs and wants
- C. Guna semua untuk keperluan, sebab kehendak tidak penting / Spend it all on needs, because wants don't matter
- D. Simpan semua RM600 dan pinjam dari kawan bila perlu / Save all RM600 and borrow from friends when needed

**2.** Apakah tujuan utama dana kecemasan?
What is the main purpose of an emergency fund?
- A. Untuk melabur dalam peluang yang menjanjikan pulangan tinggi / To invest in offers that promise high returns
- B. Untuk membeli barang semasa jualan murah / To buy things during sales
- C. Untuk kos tidak dijangka, seperti telefon rosak atau tiket pulang kecemasan / For unexpected costs, like a broken phone or an emergency trip home
- D. Untuk membayar ansuran bulanan / To pay monthly instalments

**3.** Aina mula menyimpan RM50 sebulan pada umur 19. Amir mula menyimpan RM50 sebulan pada umur 29. Kedua-duanya berhenti pada umur 55, dengan kadar contoh yang sama. Siapa ada lebih banyak duit pada umur 55?
Aina starts saving RM50 a month at 19. Amir starts saving RM50 a month at 29. Both stop at 55, at the same example rate. Who has more at 55?
- A. Amir, sebab dia lebih berpengalaman / Amir, because he is more experienced
- B. Sama banyak, sebab simpanan bulanan sama / The same, because the monthly amount is the same
- C. Tidak boleh tahu tanpa nama bank / You can't tell without the bank's name
- D. Aina, sebab duitnya ada lebih lama untuk berkembang (kompaun) / Aina, because her money has longer to grow (compounding)

**4.** Yang mana BENAR tentang pelaburan yang nilainya boleh naik dan turun (contohnya unit amanah ekuiti)?
Which is TRUE about an investment whose value can go up and down (for example an equity unit trust)?
- A. Pulangan yang mungkin lebih tinggi datang bersama risiko kehilangan sebahagian duit / A possibly higher return comes with the risk of losing some money
- B. Nilainya dijamin naik setiap bulan / Its value is guaranteed to rise every month
- C. Ia tiada risiko kalau kawan kamu juga melabur / It has no risk if your friend also invests
- D. Kalau nilainya turun sekali, duit itu hilang selamanya / If its value drops once, the money is gone forever

**5.** Kedai menawarkan telefon RM1,000 dengan "bayar nanti": kadar rata 10% setahun selama 12 bulan. Berapa jumlah yang kamu bayar semuanya?
A shop offers a RM1,000 phone on "pay later": a flat rate of 10% a year for 12 months. How much do you pay in total?
- A. RM1,000
- B. RM1,010
- C. RM1,100
- D. RM1,200

**6.** Seorang senior mengajak kamu melabur dalam satu skim. Apa langkah pertama yang paling baik?
A senior invites you to invest in a scheme. What is the best first step?
- A. Tanya berapa ramai orang sudah masuk / Ask how many people have already joined
- B. Semak sama ada syarikat dan produk itu berlesen dalam senarai rasmi pengawal selia / Check whether the company and product are licensed on the regulator's official list
- C. Mula dengan jumlah kecil dulu untuk cuba / Start with a small amount to try it
- D. Percaya, sebab senior itu kawan rapat / Trust it, because the senior is a close friend

### Bahagian B: Scam / Part B: Scams

**7.** Mesej: "Kerja sambilan! Like video, dapat RM50 sehari. Bayar RM100 dulu untuk buka komisen." Apakah ini?
Message: "Part-time job! Like videos, earn RM50 a day. Pay RM100 first to unlock your commission." What is this?
- A. Kerja sambilan yang sah / A legitimate part-time job
- B. Sah, sebab RM100 itu kecil / Legitimate, because RM100 is a small amount
- C. Sah, kalau mereka hantar bukti bayaran orang lain / Legitimate, if they send proof of other people's payments
- D. Scam: kerja sebenar tidak minta kamu bayar dulu untuk dapat gaji / A scam: a real job doesn't ask you to pay first to get paid

**8.** Seseorang tawar RM1,500 untuk "sewa" akaun bank kamu selama sebulan. Kamu cuma perlu beri kad ATM dan PIN. Apa yang betul?
Someone offers RM1,500 to "rent" your bank account for a month. You only need to hand over your ATM card and PIN. What is right?
- A. Tolak: akaun kamu boleh digunakan untuk menerima duit scam, dan kamu boleh disiasat / Refuse: your account can be used to receive scam money, and you can be investigated
- B. Terima, kalau ia untuk sebulan sahaja / Accept, if it's only for a month
- C. Terima, kalau orang itu senior di universiti / Accept, if the person is a senior at your university
- D. Terima, tapi tukar PIN selepas sebulan / Accept, but change the PIN after a month

**9.** Panggilan dari 011-0000 0123: "Saya pegawai polis. Nombor IC kamu terlibat dengan pengubahan wang haram. Pindahkan duit kamu ke akaun selamat sekarang dan jangan beritahu sesiapa." Apa yang patut kamu buat?
A call from 011-0000 0123: "I'm a police officer. Your IC number is linked to money laundering. Transfer your money to a safe account now and don't tell anyone." What should you do?
- A. Pindahkan duit dulu, kemudian semak / Transfer the money first, then check
- B. Beri nombor akaun kamu supaya mereka boleh semak / Give them your account number so they can check
- C. Letak telefon: pihak berkuasa tidak akan minta kamu pindahkan duit ke "akaun selamat" / Hang up: the authorities will never ask you to move money to a "safe account"
- D. Ikut arahan, sebab dia tahu nombor IC kamu / Follow the instructions, because they know your IC number

**10.** SMS: "Bungkusan kamu ditahan. Bayar RM2.50 dalam 24 jam di kurierkilat-bayar.test/alamat atau ia akan dipulangkan." Apa tanda bahayanya?
SMS: "Your parcel is on hold. Pay RM2.50 within 24 hours at kurierkilat-bayar.test/alamat or it will be returned." What are the warning signs?
- A. Tiada; RM2.50 terlalu kecil untuk jadi scam / None; RM2.50 is too small to be a scam
- B. Desakan masa dan pautan bayaran yang tidak dikenali; semak dalam aplikasi rasmi kurier sendiri / Time pressure and an unknown payment link; check in the courier's own official app yourself
- C. Hanya kalau ada salah ejaan / Only if there are spelling mistakes
- D. Tiada, kalau kamu memang sedang tunggu bungkusan / None, if you really are waiting for a parcel

**11.** Mesej mana yang PALING mungkin sah?
Which message is MOST likely to be legitimate?
- A. "Akaun kamu akan dibekukan hari ini. Klik pautan ini dan masukkan TAC kamu." / "Your account will be frozen today. Click this link and enter your TAC."
- B. "Tahniah! Kamu menang RM5,000. Bayar yuran proses RM200 untuk tuntut." / "Congratulations! You've won RM5,000. Pay a RM200 processing fee to claim."
- C. "Hantar gambar kad bank kamu untuk pengesahan." / "Send a photo of your bank card for verification."
- D. "Transaksi RM45.00 di Kedai Buku Cempaka. Tidak kenal transaksi ini? Buka aplikasi bank kamu sendiri untuk semak." (tiada pautan) / "RM45.00 transaction at Kedai Buku Cempaka. Don't recognise it? Open your own bank app to check." (no link)

**12.** Kamu baru sahaja memindahkan duit kepada penipu. Apa langkah pertama yang paling penting?
You have just transferred money to a scammer. What is the most important first step?
- A. Telefon NSRC 997 secepat mungkin dan beritahu bank kamu / Call NSRC 997 as soon as possible and tell your bank
- B. Tunggu sehari untuk lihat sama ada mereka pulangkan / Wait a day to see whether they return it
- C. Hantar mesej marah kepada penipu itu / Send the scammer an angry message
- D. Padam semua mesej supaya tiada siapa tahu / Delete all the messages so nobody knows

## Scoring (for D4)

- 1 mark per right answer: money score /6, scam score /6, total /12.
- For each code, record: pre money, pre scam, post money, post scam (in `results.md`).
- Report the mean before and after for each part and for the total, and n (people who took both tests).

## Known limits (state them honestly in D4)

- The same questions are used twice, so part of any gain may come from seeing them before.
- One 25-minute session; it measures short-term recall, not behaviour months later.
- The sample is small and not random (people the team knows).
- Multiple choice lets people guess (25% chance per question).
