# Problem statement (draft, ~520 characters)

Malaysians lost RM2.97 billion to online scams in 2025, almost double 2024. Young adults aged 21-30 are now
the largest victim group, and syndicates pay university students RM1,000-2,000 to "rent" their bank accounts
as mule accounts, a criminal offence many first-years don't recognise until it's too late. Current awareness
talks are one-way lectures that students quickly forget. First-year IPT students, many opening their first bank
account, need a safe way to experience scam tactics before real scammers reach them.

## Evidence (researched 5 Nov 2026)

  months of 2026 (Home Ministry).
  https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/06/24/online-scam-losses-surge-to-rm297b-in-2025-says-home-ministry-as-fraud-trend-worsens-across-malaysia/225000
- Telecom crime Jan-Nov 2025: ages 21-30 were the largest victim group (8,789 cases) (PDRM).
  https://says.com/my/news/pdrm-most-telco-scam-victims-in-malaysia-this-year-are-aged-21-to-30
- Job scams typically start as part-time job offers, then ask for a "top-up" to receive commission (Bukit Aman).
  https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/04/07/easy-part-time-jobs-now-top-tactic-in-online-scam-wave-says-bukit-aman

  Not for the pitch: outside the target group.
  https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/08/28/kids---new-frontier-in-fraud

### Mule accounts
- Syndicates offer students RM1,000-2,000 per account (Home Minister, Jun 2026).
  https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/06/17/students-turn-mule-for-cash
- 173,224 mule accounts identified, 2022 to July 2025.
  https://thesun.my/malaysia-news/over-173000-mule-accounts-identified-in-malaysia-as-of-july-2025-BE14765026
- Students made up about 8% of mule account cases (official profiling). Do NOT use the ">50%" NGO claim.
  https://newswav.com/article/rm6-7-million-recovered-for-scam-victims-as-mule-account-crackdown-intensif-A2601_SiQ1gk
- MOHE (the organiser's ministry) is expanding mule-account awareness programmes across universities.
  https://thesun.my/local-news/mule-accounts-mohe-to-expand-awareness-programmes-across-universities-mustapha-GC12895366

### Financial literacy
- Malaysians aged 19-30 consistently score below the national average in BNM's Financial Literacy and
  Capability Index.
  https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/759356
- PTPTN arrears: RM10.7 billion across 1.16 million borrowers (31 Aug 2026).
  https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/10/1551050/ptptn-borrowers-can-negotiate-loan-restructuring-deferment
- About 400,000 PTPTN borrowers have never made a single repayment.
  https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2025/06/1228717/ptptn-shock-400000-borrowers-havent-paid-sen-over-decade
- 877 youth bankruptcies in 2024, up from 727 in 2023; 5,272 aged 34 and below since 2020.
  https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/105828/minister-reveals-5272-youths-declared-bankrupt-since-2020

## Solution sketch (edutainment)
- Fake chat simulator: a "recruiter" offers an easy RM1,500 part-time job or asks to borrow your bank account;
  every reply choice has consequences (frozen account, police report, money lost).
- Red-flag collection: students unlock a "red flag card" for each tactic they spot.
- Adaptive scenarios: track which tactics each student falls for and make those harder next time.
- Money life simulator (financial literacy layer): first semester budget, PTPTN, BNPL, and the cost of falling
  for a scam.
- Validation idea: before/after test with UTP first-years ("70% fell for the fake job before, 20% after").
